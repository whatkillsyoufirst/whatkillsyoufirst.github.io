Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026

In short: the app only ever connects to the channel's own accounts, keeps login tokens on the owner's own computer, and collects nothing about anyone else. This website itself uses no cookies, analytics or tracking.

What data the app accesses

When the channel owner connects the channel's TikTok account, TikTok gives the app permission for two things:

  • Basic account information (user.info.basic): the account's display name and identifier, used to confirm the right account is connected.
  • Video publishing (video.publish): permission to upload the channel's own videos and captions to that account.

What the app stores, and where

The app stores one thing for each connected platform: the access token issued after login, so it can post without asking to log in every time. Tokens are kept in files on the owner's own computer. They are not uploaded to any server, shared, or sold.

What the app does not do

  • It does not access other people's accounts, followers' data, messages, or viewing activity.
  • It does not use cookies, analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • It does not sell or share any data with third parties.

Deleting data

Access can be removed at any time by revoking the app in the platform's settings (on TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions) and deleting the stored token file. After that, the app has no access and keeps no data.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not collect information from anyone other than the channel's owner.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
